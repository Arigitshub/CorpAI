# CorpAI Automation Revenue Plan

## Goal

Turn CorpAI into a product that can produce recurring revenue by automating narrow business workflows first, then expanding into wider AI business units.

## Core thesis

CorpAI should not be sold first as a protocol for autonomous corporations.

It should be sold as:

- AI support labor with oversight
- AI operations labor with oversight
- AI growth labor with oversight
- AI reporting labor with oversight

The protocol is the infrastructure.
The offer is the workflow.

## What "automatic money" should mean here

Not magic.
Not passive income fiction.

It should mean:

- automated lead qualification
- automated proposal preparation
- automated delivery for repeatable workflows
- automated reporting that justifies renewals and expansions

That is how CorpAI compounds into recurring revenue.

## Product ladder

### 1. Pilot

Price range:

- $1k-$3k per month

Offer:

- one narrow workflow
- one dashboard
- one approval chain
- one buyer persona

Examples:

- support inbox triage
- weekly founder report generation
- lead research and enrichment

### 2. Business Unit

Price range:

- $4k-$12k per month

Offer:

- a packaged AI team for one business function
- reusable templates
- escalation and human approval
- reporting on hours saved, tasks handled, and outcomes produced

Examples:

- Support OS
- Ops OS
- Growth OS

### 3. Managed Workforce

Price range:

- $15k+ per month

Offer:

- multiple workflows under one CorpAI deployment
- managed tuning and playbook changes
- weekly reviews
- expansion into adjacent workflows

## First workflow to sell

Support OS.

Why:

- the pain is obvious
- ROI is easier to explain
- escalation is natural
- quality can be reviewed by humans
- recurring volume is common

## Revenue loop

### Acquire

- AI researches target accounts
- AI qualifies inbound leads
- AI drafts outreach and tailored demos

### Convert

- AI builds discovery summaries
- AI drafts proposals and ROI snapshots
- human closes and approves

### Deliver

- CorpAI roles run the workflow
- escalation goes to a human only when needed
- outputs are logged and summarized automatically

### Retain

- AI sends weekly reports
- AI surfaces wins, risks, and expansion opportunities
- renewals and upsells become easier to justify

## Metrics that matter

- monthly recurring revenue
- gross margin by workflow
- hours replaced or redirected
- approval rate
- escalation rate
- task completion time
- renewal rate
- expansion revenue

## Product rule

Do not ship broad autonomy first.

Ship repeatable, supervised business workflows that can be sold, renewed, and expanded.
