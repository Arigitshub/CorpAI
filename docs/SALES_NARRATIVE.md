# Sales Narrative

## One-sentence pitch

CorpAI is the control plane for teams running AI agents across Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Claude Code, OpenRouter, and multi-model workflows.

## Supporting pitch

Your team already uses coding agents. CorpAI makes that usage governable, measurable, and repeatable with policy, routing, approvals, audit logs, workflow templates, and spend visibility.

## Homepage messaging blocks

### Headline

- Govern every AI agent run across your team.

### Subheadline

- One place to route models, enforce policy, review outputs, track spend, and reuse proven workflows across your engineering organization.

### Proof blocks

- Policy
- Routing
- Visibility

### CTA options

- Book an AgentOps audit
- Run a managed pilot
- See the control plane demo

## What not to say

Do not lead with:

- autonomous corporation
- build any AI workforce
- wrapper around Codex
- replace your company
- protocol-first language

## Objection handling frame

If a buyer says "we already use Codex/Gemini/Claude," the answer is:

- that is exactly why CorpAI exists
- the runtime is not the control plane
- CorpAI standardizes how your team uses those runtimes across repos, policies, reviews, and budgets
