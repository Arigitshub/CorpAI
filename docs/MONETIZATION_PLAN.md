# CorpAI Monetization Plan

## Goal

Turn CorpAI into a product that can generate recurring revenue by packaging supervised AI labor into repeatable systems.

## Core monetization paths

### 1. AI workforce subscriptions

Sell packaged AI teams for:

- customer support
- operations
- growth
- reporting

Customers pay monthly for the outcome and the control layer.

The model backbone can run through:

- CorpAI-managed provider accounts
- OpenRouter where routing and fallback help
- direct provider APIs where margin or quality is better
- enterprise BYOK later for larger accounts

### 2. Template packs

Sell industry-specific packs such as:

- ecommerce support org
- agency ops org
- founder office org
- outbound growth org

These reduce setup time and increase perceived value.

### 3. Managed automation retainers

Use CorpAI as the operating layer for done-for-you services.

This is likely the fastest path to revenue because it avoids waiting for pure self-serve adoption.

## Billing architecture

The billing model should have three layers:

1. base platform fee
2. managed workflow fee
3. usage overage or vendor pass-through

That allows CorpAI to preserve margin while still using third-party model infrastructure underneath.

## Best first product

The best first product is probably `Support OS`.

Why:

- clear pain
- easy ROI story
- simpler approval model
- easier to demo than a broad autonomous company concept

## What customers should buy first

Do not sell “the full autonomous corporation” first.

Sell:

- an AI support team
- an AI ops team
- an AI reporting system

Those are easier to understand, easier to pilot, and easier to renew.

Do not sell:

- raw model access
- seats
- an undifferentiated CLI wrapper

## Product principle

The infrastructure can stay broad.
The offer should stay narrow.
