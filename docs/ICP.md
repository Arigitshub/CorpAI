# ICP

## Primary ICP

CorpAI should first target technical teams already using agentic tooling in real work.

Best first segments:

- AI-native software agencies
- startup engineering teams
- internal platform or developer productivity teams

## Best first buyer profile

Target teams with:

- 8 to 80 engineers or technical operators
- multiple active repositories
- existing use of `Codex CLI`, `Gemini CLI`, `Claude Code`, OpenRouter, or direct APIs
- visible pain around policy, review, spend, or standardization

## Economic buyer

- founder
- CTO
- VP Engineering
- Head of Platform
- Head of Developer Experience

## Champion

- engineering manager
- staff engineer
- platform engineer
- AI automation lead
- technical operations lead

## Disqualifiers

Avoid first:

- hobbyists and solo developers
- broad non-technical SMBs
- slow enterprise-first procurement motions
- teams not already using coding agents at all
- buyers who want pure self-serve from day one

## First buyer sentence

The ideal first buyer already believes coding agents are useful and now needs a control plane to make them safe, measurable, and repeatable.
