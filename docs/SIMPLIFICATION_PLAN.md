# CorpAI Simplification Plan

## Problem

CorpAI currently explains its protocol depth before it explains the business outcome.

That makes the project impressive to technical readers, but slower to understand for buyers.

## Simpler story

CorpAI should be positioned as:

- an AI operating system for business workflows
- a command center for supervised AI teams
- a reusable structure for automating support, operations, growth, and reporting

## What should stay

- the rank system
- escalation and governance
- reusable role definitions
- the spec itself as the backbone

## What should change

- lead the website with the business result, not the standard
- show narrow product wedges before ecosystem ambition
- explain human oversight clearly
- explain what a customer is actually buying

## Product wedge

The first buyer-friendly wedge is not “AI org charts.”

It is:

- Support OS
- Ops OS
- Growth OS

Each of these is easier to buy than a general-purpose agent hierarchy standard.

## Website rule

A new visitor should understand these four things in under 30 seconds:

1. what CorpAI automates
2. what humans still approve
3. which business function it replaces or accelerates
4. why that creates ROI
